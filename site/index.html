Good questions

Honest answers

Why $4.20? +
Because it is a fair price for a small tool that does one thing well, and because the number makes us smile. The real point: it is one payment, not a monthly bill. Buy it, own it, done.
Is my voice uploaded anywhere? +
Never. Every speech engine runs on your Mac, either Apple's on-device Speech framework or a Whisper or Moonshine model running locally. There is no server, no account, no cloud transcript. The simplest proof is that it keeps working with your internet turned off. If audio were leaving your machine, offline dictation would be impossible.
Does it really work offline? +
Yes. Apple Speech works out of the box. The Whisper and Moonshine models download once (in the background, with Apple Speech covering you meanwhile), and after that everything is local. Plane mode, dead wifi, air-gapped machine, it all keeps working.
Which speech engine should I use? +
Start with Apple Speech, it is instant and needs no download. If you want higher accuracy, especially for long-form or accents, download a Whisper model from the menu. Whisper Small is the sweet spot for most people, and the Large v3 Turbo models are the most accurate. You can switch anytime from the menu bar.
How accurate is it compared to Apple's built-in dictation? +
Apple Speech uses the same underlying framework as macOS built-in dictation, so accuracy is comparable, and instant. The difference is choice: with a Whisper model selected, accuracy on longer or trickier speech, technical vocabulary, or accented voices is noticeably better. And unlike Apple's system dictation, you decide which engine runs and you are never nudged toward an online mode.
Is this on the Mac App Store? +
No, and by design. SimpleDictation drops your words into other apps through the clipboard and paste, which needs the Accessibility permission and is not allowed inside the App Store sandbox. Selling it directly means it can do the thing you actually want it to do. Setup is simple: download the DMG, drag SimpleDictation to your Applications folder, and open it. v2 is notarized and Developer ID signed, so it opens with no security warning. When you launch it, grant the three permissions it asks for (Microphone, Accessibility, and Speech Recognition) and you are set.
How does it get text into other apps? +
When you release the key, SimpleDictation puts the transcript on your clipboard and pastes it at your cursor. That is why it works in Slack, Notion, VS Code, address bars, and almost anywhere else without any per-app integration. It also means the Accessibility permission is required, and that the clipboard history feature comes along for free.
Can I use it on more than one Mac? +
Yes. Your license key does a single online activation the one time you enter it, then the app verifies it locally and works fully offline after that. There is no ongoing tracking, no account, and your voice is never uploaded. One key activates a couple of your Macs, so install it wherever you work.
